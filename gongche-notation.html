Notation System Research

Traditional Chinese Music Notation Digital Project

Computer digital font development of the Gongche notation system.

ProjectTraditional Chinese Music Notation Digital Project
SystemGongche notation
ClientCulture and Art Publishing House
PeriodSept, 2020 to Sept, 2024
ScopeDigital font development, grapheme tables, beat symbols, combining layouts, horizontal and vertical layout tests

Gongche notation is one of the main methods of recording traditional music in China. The project studies its pitch characters, octave variants, beat symbols, and complex combining layouts as the basis for computer digital typeface development.
